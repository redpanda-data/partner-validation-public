Model provenance (validated Redpanda-on-OCI benchmark campaigns, Feb–Jul 2026): NIC = 1 Gbps/OCPU full duplex (OCI DenseIO spec). Wire accounting: IN = P·c·RF, OUT = P·c·(RF−1) + P·c·F. CPU busy-cores = α·P + β·(RF−1)·P + γ·P·F, defaults fitted to: 10 GB/s RF=1 (8× E4-32 @77% CPU, Jul 13) and 3 GB/s RF=3 (4× E4-32 @69% CPU measured Jul 23, p99 7.5–7.8 ms). Latency guidance from the measured ladder: fsync acks=all ≈29 ms; write_caching/acks=0 ≈1.5–8 ms, degrading as CPU/NIC exceed ~65–70%. Compression ratio c applies to wire+disk (producer-side compression; 1.0=none, lz4 typical 0.3–0.6 on JSON, ~0.25 on repetitive payloads). Broker floor: RF≥2 needs ≥RF+1 nodes to self-heal after a broker loss. Object storage: tiered uploads 1 logical copy regardless of RF. Prices are editable estimates — validate against your rate card.
